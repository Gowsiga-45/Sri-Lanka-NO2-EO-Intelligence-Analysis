02 // KEY JUDGMENTS

Analytical Assessment

Judgments integrate satellite-derived observations with supporting open-source evidence. Confidence reflects the strength and consistency of the available evidence.

KJ-01 HIGH CONFIDENCE

Colombo was the dominant persistent NO₂ hotspot.

Colombo recorded the highest district mean tropospheric NO₂ column density and the greatest persistent-hotspot coverage in the district-level analysis.

KJ-02 MODERATE CONFIDENCE

The Western Province pattern is consistent with substantial anthropogenic influence.

EO observations and supporting open-source evidence are consistent with contributions from urban mobility, transportation and industrial activity. Available evidence does not permit reliable attribution to a single emission source.

KJ-03 HIGH CONFIDENCE

The 2020 NO₂ signal was temporally variable.

Independent observations documenting substantial NO₂ reductions during COVID-19 restrictions indicate that changes in human activity materially influenced Colombo's atmospheric NO₂ conditions during the assessment period.

KJ-04 MODERATE CONFIDENCE

Source-specific attribution remains unresolved.

Transport, industrial and other urban emission sources remain plausible contributors. Additional source-specific emissions, meteorological and ground-observation data would be required for stronger attribution.
